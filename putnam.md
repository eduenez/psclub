The William Lowell Putnam Mathematical Competition is a North American undergraduate
mathematics competition, entered every December by students at
several hundred institutions. UTSA has been participating yearly since 2006.
The Problem-Solving Club fall sessions emphasize Putnam preparation.

Please consider **registering now**.

**UTSA's registration deadline is Thursday 3 December 2026.**
The national organizers do not set one; this deadline is ours, so that we can
arrange a room and materials before the Saturday.

**Note:** Sign-up *must* be done individually using the link above.
Sign-in sheets for PS Club do *not* serve as registration.

## Putnam Competition date

**Saturday 5 December 2026, 9:00 a.m. to 6:15 p.m.** 

The competition format is changing this year. 
The exam now runs as **four ninety-minute sessions** with breaks between them.
During each session, students may *not* leave the room and return.

## Registration eligibility

Undergraduates who have *not* yet received a 4-year college degree. 
All majors are eligible. (Any student may take the Putnam at most four times.)

### Really, (almost) all students are eligible!

Do *not* “wait until next year” to be “better prepared” to take the test.
Start your *freshman year*, and repeat yearly up to 4 times total.
*Every one* of UTSA‘s top-scoring students took the exam multiple times,
and the first time was never the best one 
—but each of them started taking the Putnam their very first year
in the PS Club.
Several UTSA students have taken the Putnam the maximum 4-time limit.

## How the club prepares

We meet weekly and work problems together, and several of the sets this term
close with past Putnam problems (typically among the easier A1, A2,
B1 and B2). 
Past sets are on the [problem sets page]({{ '/problems/' | relative_url }}).
The [mailing list]({{ '/join/' | relative_url }}) carries the deadline
reminders.

*Note:* Attending PS Club meetings is encouraged but not required to enter the Putnam.
